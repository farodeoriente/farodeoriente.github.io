About us

A lighthouse that points east

东 方 灯 塔

Lighthouses guide ships along the Galician coast. Ours points east, towards China: a steady light for anyone setting out to learn Chinese.

Our teachers

Five native Chinese teachers with degrees from BA to PhD. Some hold international certificates for teaching Chinese as a foreign language. Between them they have 4 to 20 years of teaching experience, and they all teach in Chinese, Spanish and English.

How we teach

Small classes

Every student speaks in every lesson.

Clear levels

Courses follow A1–A2 and the HSK levels, so progress is easy to see.

Language with culture

Festivals, calligraphy, stories and history are part of the course.

Three classroom languages

Teachers explain in Spanish or English when needed.

How we work

We provide the teachers and the curriculum; local schools and academies provide the classrooms and the students. In China, our partner Connect China organises our summer programmes.

Why “Faro de Oriente”

Galicia is a land of lighthouses, and Santiago is where the Camino ends. We wanted a name that joins the two shores: a Galician lighthouse with its light turned towards China.
