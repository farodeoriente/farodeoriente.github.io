Groups by age and level

Questions about this course

Does my child need to speak Chinese?
Classes are taught in Chinese, for children who hear Chinese at home. Children are grouped by age and level.
Can my child join during the year?
Yes. Message Elva on WhatsApp or WeChat to book a place.

Rúa de Ramón Cabanillas, 10 · 15701 Santiago de Compostela

Yes. Send Elva a message and she'll arrange a trial day.
