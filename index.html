Every course follows the same levels (A1–A2, HSK 1–2), so you always know where you are and what comes next.

柒

Frequently asked questions

Do we need to know any Chinese already?
No. The evening courses start from zero. At the weekend school, children are grouped by age and level.
From what age can children start?
From 5 years old. There are also groups for teenagers (10–17) and adults (18+).
Which language do the teachers use in class?
At the weekend school, Chinese. In the evening courses, English or Spanish. Online lessons can be in any of the three.
Can I try a lesson first?
Yes, trial lessons are available. Send us a WhatsApp message and we'll arrange one.
Are the summer trips suitable for teenagers?
Yes. Teenagers (14–17) and adults (18+) travel in separate groups, with one guide for every 10 participants and a group carer with every group.
