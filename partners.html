Who brings what

Faro de Oriente provides

  • Native Chinese teachers (BA to PhD, 4–20 years of experience)
  • Curriculum and materials for kids, teens and adults (A1–A2)
  • Teaching quality and progress reports
  • Optional: summer study trips to China for your students

Your school provides

  • Classrooms and timetable
  • Student recruitment and enrolment
  • Local promotion and contact with families
